Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Duped is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the game keeps

Your progress (daily results, streak, best endless score) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device and usage information such as IP address, device model, ad interactions, and crash and performance data.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown.
  • On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you say no, you still get the full game with non-personalized ads.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.
