Privacy Policy

Duped: Don't Trust the Button · Effective 20 November 2026

The short version. Duped has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising service (Google AdMob) collects to show ads.

Who we are

Duped is published by Muhammed Kizilkaya ("we", "us"). You can reach us at info.muhammedkizilkaya@gmail.com.

What the game itself stores

The game saves the following on your device only:

  • your daily results and number of tries,
  • your streak and your best Endless score,
  • how many times the app has been opened.

This information is never sent to us. We cannot see it, and we do not have a copy of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone, and it has no sign-up or login.

Advertising

Duped is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. There are two kinds: a video you can choose to watch to continue a run, and occasional full-screen ads between finished runs.

To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (on iOS only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location,
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them,
  • device and app information, and diagnostics such as crash and performance data.

Google acts under its own privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking on iOS. The game asks for permission before any tracking. If you say no, the game works exactly the same and ads are not based on tracking you across other apps and websites. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in Europe and the UK. Where the law requires it, you are shown a consent form before personalised ads are used. You can decline.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete your game data. Uninstall the app. There is nothing else to delete, because we hold no data about you.

Sharing your result

If you tap the share button, the game creates an image of your result on your device and opens your device's share sheet. The image goes only where you choose to send it. We do not receive it.

Children

Duped is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google. If you write to us, we will help you find the right place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
