Common questions

Why is today's puzzle the same for everyone? The daily level is made from the date, so everyone on Earth gets the same ten traps and can compare results.

I lost my streak. Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app resets it.

Can I play without ads? Ads only appear between finished runs, never during one, and the optional "watch to continue" video is always your choice.
