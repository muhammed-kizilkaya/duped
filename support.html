Common questions

How do I play?

There is one button and one instruction. Read the instruction carefully, then tap, or don't. The instruction is often a trap: the button's label may say the opposite, the rule may change at the last second, or you may be asked about something you saw a few steps earlier.

What is the difference between Daily and Endless?

Daily is a set of 10 traps that is the same for everyone that day. Clear it on consecutive days to build a streak. Endless keeps sending traps, faster each time, until you fail. Your best score is saved.

Why did my streak reset?

A streak counts consecutive days on which you cleared the Daily. Missing a day resets it to zero.

I retried the Daily and the traps were in a different order. Is that a bug?

No. Your first try each day is the shared Daily. Retries use the same kinds of traps but reshuffle the order and the numbers, so you cannot just memorise the answers.

Why can't I continue after failing?

You can continue once per run by watching a video ad. If the button shows "Loading ad…", no ad is available yet: check your internet connection and try again in a moment. After you have continued once, the option is not offered again in the same run.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Only ads need an internet connection.

Can I remove the ads?

Not at the moment. The video ad only plays when you choose it, and full-screen ads only appear between finished runs.

I got a new phone. Can I move my progress?

Progress is stored only on your device and there are no accounts, so it does not transfer automatically. It may come along if you restore your new phone from a full device backup.

How do I reset my progress?

Delete the app and install it again.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, Spanish, and Portuguese. The game follows your device language.
